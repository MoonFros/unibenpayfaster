Payment guide

A few things to know.

Clear guidance helps make every payment journey more confident.

Explore the payment form ↗
How do I know which account to use?+

In the proposed flow, the destination is matched to your selected payment category. Confirm all details through official UNIBEN Microfinance channels before any real transfer.

When will my receipt be available?+

A receipt would only be issued after payment verification by authorized personnel. The preview on this page is illustrative and is not evidence of payment.

Can I use this site to make a payment now?+

No. This is a non-functional visual prototype. It has no payment gateway, does not initiate transfers and uses fictional account details.

What should I do if I need payment support?+

Please use the university's official contact channels. Do not share PINs, passwords or one-time verification codes with anyone.

Questions about the proposal?

Contact the UNIBEN Microfinance Bank through official university channels for verified payment information and support.
